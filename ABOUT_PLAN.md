## Naming recommendation
The long brief is mostly the park's story (history timeline, playground, movies, community). The nav already has "Our Story" and "About / Contact".
- Page 1: "Our Story" (/our-story/, ID 120): hero, What is Box H, "This Park Runs on Community", timeline 1980-2024, Today, Box H Difference cards, Playground Story + gallery, Movies Under the Stars, community photos, Our Future, link to History Archive.

Reason: one page would be very long on phones; the story and the "who/how to reach us" have different jobs. Alternative: single page called "About Box H Park" and drop "Our Story" from the menu.

## CMS requirements (free approach)
- Board members: custom post type "Board Member" (name, position, photo, bio, community role, current/past flag). Free: Custom Post Type UI + ACF free or Meta Box, or plain posts in a "Board" category rendered by a list shortcode. Do not hardcode names from news articles.
- Partners: custom post type "Partner" (name, logo, website, contribution/project, year, description); board-controlled list only.
- Displaying CPT grids without Elementor Pro Loop Grid: use a free listing plugin/shortcode or ElementsKit Lite widgets (check availability). If this becomes a blocker, revisit (owner still resisting Pro).
